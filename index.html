FaceDefense Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Overview

FaceDefense is a gamified habit-tracking app that works in tandem with a physical wrist-worn accelerometer device to monitor and break harmful hand-to-face motions (such as skin picking, nail-biting, and eye-rubbing). This policy explains what data the app handles, how it is stored, and your rights. Your movement and habit data remains entirely on your local device and physical sensor—the app operates offline without mandatory external cloud sync.

No Account Required

FaceDefense does not require you to create an account or provide personal identifying information like your name, email address, or phone number. You can fully use the app's features, level up, and earn rewards without revealing your identity.

What Data the App Uses

  • Movement & Accelerometer Data: FaceDefense reads real-time 3D motion data from your wrist sensor via a local Bluetooth connection to detect hand-to-face movements.
  • Custom Gesture Profiles: Custom habit calibrations created in the Custom Gesture Lab are saved directly on your smartphone to train your device's detection accuracy.
  • Session History & Analytics: Daily touch counts, hot zone times, and quest progress are stored locally on your device or the hardware microcontroller. This data is never uploaded to external servers or accessible to us.

Permissions We Use

  • Bluetooth: Required strictly to pair with and stream real-time accelerometer readings from your wrist-worn sensor.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used locally to send game alerts, streak reminders, and quest updates.

FaceDefense does not request access to your camera, microphone, or precise location.

Third-Party Services

FaceDefense operates as a standalone application. No analytics platforms, third-party advertising networks, or external cloud storage services are used to harvest or track your movement data.

How to Delete Your Data

You can permanently erase all recorded motion history, habit logs, level progress, and custom gesture profiles at any time. Navigate to Settings > Account & Privacy and tap Clear All Data. This action takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

Children's Privacy

FaceDefense is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal data is transmitted or collected, no special parental consent procedures are required.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be published within the app and via the App Store listing. Continued use of FaceDefense following any updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

Important Notice & Disclaimer

For Informational & Behavioral Training Purposes Only

FaceDefense is a gamified personal wellness tool designed to assist with Habit Reversal Training (HRT) and bring awareness to subconscious physical habits. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any dermatological, psychological, or neurological condition (such as dermatillomania, trichotillomania, or severe compulsive disorders).

Accuracy of Motion Detection

Touch detection scores, gesture breakdowns, and hot zone graphs are estimates derived from physical accelerometer readings. Detection accuracy can be influenced by wrist placement, sudden body movements, or external vibrations. Results are intended for casual tracking and may not reflect every movement with clinical precision. If you suffer from severe skin trauma or underlying health conditions, consult a qualified medical professional.

The developer is not liable for any injury, skin irritation, habit persistence, or damage resulting from your use of FaceDefense or reliance on the app's gamification features. Use of this app is entirely at your own risk.

Hardware Dependency

FaceDefense requires a compatible wrist-worn accelerometer sensor to function. The app's tracking ability depends on correct hardware placement, proper Bluetooth pairing, and sensor calibration. The developer makes no guarantee that the app will perform accurately with uncalibrated, damaged, or non-compatible hardware.
